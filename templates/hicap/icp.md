# Hicap ideal customer profile

Hicap sells a shared AI control layer: per-key budgets, rate limits, model
allow-lists, exportable audit trails, and per-call cost attribution.

Prioritize established fintech, insurtech, enterprise SaaS, and vertical
software companies serving non-technical industries. Look for companies
retrofitting AI across multiple teams or providers, with an accountable owner,
a specific unresolved budget/access/audit/attribution problem, and evidence that
they buy external infrastructure instead of building everything internally.

AI-native builders are a negative prior, not an automatic exclusion. Unknown is
not false. Never infer purchase intent from industry alone.

Target roles:

- Head or VP of Platform Engineering
- VP Engineering or CTO
- Head of AI or ML Platform
- CISO or Security Engineering
- Head of FinOps or Cloud Economics
